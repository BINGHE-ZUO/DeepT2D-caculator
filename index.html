What drives this person's risk

Local version (optional)

Absolute risk is only reliable where the model has been calibrated. A health system with its own follow-up data can create a local version with 3R (rescale, refine, rebaseline) and load its parameter file here. Nothing is uploaded; the file is read in this browser.

The model was developed in UK Biobank participants in England and validated, without any change, in six other populations. Each can be used as the reference group for ranking a person's risk. Choose a cohort to see who took part, how diabetes was recorded and how well the original model worked there.

Median (interquartile range) or mean (SD), as in table 1 of the paper. Cases are incident type 2 diabetes over all follow-up; the five year counts are those used for validation.

Research use only. This prototype is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Values are processed in this browser and are not stored or sent anywhere.
Rankings and the comparison with HbA1c ≥5.7% were validated in the UK, the USA and China. Absolute five year risk from the original model was accurate in England (observed to expected ratio 1.04 in ELSA) but too low in Scotland and Wales, the USA and China, so it is shown only when an English reference group or a local version is selected.
